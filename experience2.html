01 Your Visit

From hello to the mirror.

  1. 01

    Pull up a chair.

    You’ll be greeted by name and offered something to sip: coffee, Southern sweet tea or a seasonal mimosa. Get comfortable. This is your time.

  2. 02

    Talk it through.

    Every service starts with a real conversation about your hair history, your routine and the look you’re dreaming of. Extensions and big blonding changes begin with a short consultation before booking.

    Consultation first
  3. 03

    The mix.

    Color is mixed for you and only you, never one-size-fits-all. Dimensional lived-in blonde, a root refresh or a glassy gloss, it’s tailored to your tone.

    Humidity-proofed

03 Guidelines

A few kindrequests.

They keep every appointment calm, on time and entirely about you.

All extensions and heavy custom blonding services require a brief digital consultation form before booking. This makes sure the exact time your hair deserves is set aside.

Your appointment time is reserved just for you. Please give at least 48 hours’ notice for any cancellation or reschedule.

To protect the focus of every guest’s appointment, arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule or adjust their service.

Relax, unwind and settle in. Coffee, sweet tea, seasonal mimosas and curated snacks are always waiting at your chair.
