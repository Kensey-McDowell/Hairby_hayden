01 Meet Hayden

Hey y'all, I'm Hayden.

Born and raised with a deep love for true Southern hospitality, I've always believed a salon should feel like coming home, just with better lighting and a really good blowout.

So that's what I bring to every appointment: real comfort, upscale and premium execution, and all the time your hair deserves.

It's not just about a temporary style. It's about crafting a look that stays seamless, healthy and stunning weeks after you leave my chair.

  1. i.

    Southern roots

    Hospitality isn't a policy here, it's how I was raised. You'll be greeted by name, handed something cold to sip, and never rushed.

  2. ii.

    The craft

    I specialize in dimensional, lived-in blondes and luxury hand-tied extension transformations: color and length that look like they grew that way.

  3. iii.

    Where to find me

    These days I’m behind the chair at The Look Beauty Bar in Murfreesboro, Tennessee. Every appointment is one-on-one with me, from the first consultation to the final flip.

02 The Philosophy

Great hair is born from evolution, not limitation. Every cut is mapped to you: your face, your texture, your mornings. And every one is finished with the kind of care your grandmother would call proper.

Whether you're after structural precision, seamlessly lived-in highlights or a full color transformation, the goal never changes: an unhurried, uncompromised luxury experience that still looks like you weeks after you leave the chair.
