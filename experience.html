Meet Hayden

Born and raised with a deep love for true Southern hospitality, I wanted to build a salon environment that balances comfort with upscale, premium execution.

I specialize in dimensional, lived-in blondes and luxury hand-tied extension transformations. To me, it's not just about a temporary style—it's about crafting custom looks that stay seamless, healthy, and stunning weeks after you leave my chair.

Studio Guidelines

New Guest Consultations

All extensions and heavy custom blonding services require a brief digital consultation form prior to booking. This ensures we allocate the exact custom timing your hair deserves.

Cancellation Policy

Your appointment space is reserved exclusively for you. Out of respect for my scheduling, please provide at least 48 hours notice for any cancellations or rescheduling requests.

Late Arrivals

To preserve the precision and dedicated focus of every guest’s experience, arrivals more than 15 minutes late may require rescheduling or an altered custom service scope.

The Studio Vibe

Relax, unwind, and settle in. Complimentary high-end coffee, Southern sweet tea, seasonal mimosas, and curated snacks are always waiting for you at your chair.
